Draw on Screen

Support for the macOS drawing overlay.

Account and sign-in

Sign in with Google, Apple or email/password before using the drawing tools. Use Reset Password to recover an email/password account. The account screen supports English and Russian. Deleting the account from Account > Delete Account affects all participating Sergannn apps.

Help

For sign-in, drawing, purchase or account-deletion questions, email su.vasiliev@gmail.com. Include your macOS version, app version and what happened. Do not send your password.

Privacy policy
