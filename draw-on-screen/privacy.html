Draw on Screen Privacy

Updated November 20, 2026.

Shared account

The app requires sign-in through Google, Apple or email/password using Firebase Authentication. Your account ID, email and provider profile information are used to sign you in and manage your account. Authentication security processing may include IP addresses, device information and sign-in events. The same account is available in participating Sergannn apps. See the Shared Account Privacy Policy for provider information and your account rights.

Drawing and diagnostics

Drawing strokes are handled locally on your Mac. The sign-in service does not upload your drawings or screen contents. The app uses Sentry to report technical diagnostics and launch events, which can include an installation identifier and technical environment information. These help identify errors and improve the app. See Sentry Privacy.

Account deletion

Open Account and choose Delete Account. Confirm your original sign-in method to delete the shared Firebase account. This removes sign-in access across participating Sergannn apps, not only Draw on Screen. Local drawing data and independently stored service records are not automatically erased by this action.
