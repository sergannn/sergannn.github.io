Coffee Money Support & Privacy

Coffee Money is a simple local tracker for coffee spending and daily coffee habits.

Support

If you need help with Coffee Money, contact support by email:

Privacy Policy

Coffee Money stores coffee entries locally on the user's device.

  • The app does not require an account.
  • The app does not collect personal data.
  • The app does not upload coffee entries to a server.
  • The app does not use advertising tracking.

Optional local notifications are scheduled on the device and are used only for reminders.
