Privacy

Last updated: November 20, 2026.

Data

Piano Chords Quiz requires a Sergannn account. You can sign in using Google, Apple, or an email address and password. Authentication is provided by Google Firebase Authentication. We use your account ID, email address and, when provided by your sign-in service, name and profile information to create and manage your account. Passwords are handled by the authentication service and are not displayed to us.

This account is shared across participating Sergannn apps. Signing in does not by itself grant access to private data or administrator roles in another service. Firebase processes authentication and security information, such as IP addresses and sign-in activity, to operate and protect the service. See Firebase Privacy.

The app may send technical diagnostics and usage events to help improve stability. We do not use account information for cross-app advertising tracking or sell it to advertisers.

Account deletion

Open Account and choose Delete account to delete your shared Firebase account. This removes sign-in access across participating Sergannn apps. Data kept separately by an individual service is not automatically deleted by this action; contact support to request its deletion. You can also request account deletion by emailing the address below.

No sensitive content

The app is a music education tool. It does not ask for health, financial, contact, or location data.

Support

If you contact support by email, your message and email address are used only to reply to your request.
