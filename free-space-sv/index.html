Free Space SV

A compact macOS monitor for storage and memory numbers that should stay visible without opening System Settings.

About

Free Space SV keeps a small status window on your desktop with available storage, RAM, warning levels, recent changes, and optional storage-source details.

  • Floating status window for free storage and RAM.
  • Configurable warning thresholds.
  • Optional last-change and likely-source details.
  • Manual storage scan from settings.
