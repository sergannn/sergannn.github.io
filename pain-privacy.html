Privacy Policy

Pain Statistics is designed as a personal pain diary. In the current version, user entries are stored locally on the device to provide diary history, statistics, and CSV export.

Information the app uses

The app may store the following user-provided content on the device:

  • pain attack timestamps
  • optional notes added by the user
  • language preference selected in the app

Data sharing

The current version of the app does not require creating an account and does not provide its core diary functionality through a remote server. Data is not sold.
