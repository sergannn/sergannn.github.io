Афиша концертов СПб

A concert calendar for Saint Petersburg classical music venues, with dates, venues, event details, and concert programs when available.

About

The app helps listeners find upcoming concerts in Saint Petersburg without manually checking many venue websites.

  • Calendar of upcoming concerts.
  • Venue filters.
  • Event details, performers, and programs when available.
  • Ticket or event page links when source venues provide them.

Support

If you need help with the app or notice outdated event data, contact su.vasiliev@gmail.com.

Privacy

The app does not require an account. It displays public concert information and opens venue pages for ticket details.
