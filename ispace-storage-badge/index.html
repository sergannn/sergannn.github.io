iSpace Storage Badge

A small iPhone and iPad utility that shows available device storage and can display it as a number on the app icon badge.

21,444

About

Open the app to refresh free space, enable monitoring, and set a low-storage alert threshold. The badge shows rounded free GB, or MB when free space is below 1 GB.

  • Current available storage in GB or MB.
  • App icon badge with the latest available-space value.
  • Optional local notification when storage is low.
  • No account required.

Support

If you need help with iSpace Storage Badge, contact su.vasiliev@gmail.com.

Please include your device model, iOS version, and a screenshot if the badge number looks unexpected.

Privacy

The app works locally on your device. It does not require an account and does not upload storage information to a server.

Local notifications are scheduled on the device only when you enable them.
