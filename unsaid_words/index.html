Unsaid Words

A private place for anger, thanks, apologies, memories, and words you cannot send yet.

Support

For support, contact vseznayka@mail.ru.

Unsaid Words works without an account. Write a private note, choose its feeling, save it for later, release it, or share the text if you decide to send it.

Privacy Policy

Unsaid Words does not collect, sell, or share personal data.

  • Notes are stored locally on your device.
  • Notes are not uploaded to a server.
  • No account, analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking is used.

Data Deletion

You can delete any note inside the app. Removing the app from your device removes its local data.
