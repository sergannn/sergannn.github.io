Pain Statistics

Support

Pain Statistics is a simple pain diary app that helps users log pain attacks, add optional notes, review recent history, and export records as CSV.

For support, questions, or feedback, please contact su.vasiliev@gmail.com.

What the app does

  • Logs pain attacks in one tap
  • Lets users add notes to each record
  • Shows history grouped by day
  • Displays simple statistics and trends
  • Exports records as CSV
