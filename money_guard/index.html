Money Guard

A private reminder app for subscriptions, trials, returns, and warranties.

Support

For support, contact vseznayka@mail.ru.

Money Guard works without an account. Add a money date, optional amount, optional photo, and schedule a local reminder before the deadline.

Privacy Policy

Money Guard does not collect, sell, or share personal data.

  • Entries are stored locally on your device.
  • Receipt and screenshot text recognition uses on-device Apple Vision OCR.
  • Images are not uploaded to a server.
  • No account, analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking is used.

Data Deletion

You can delete any reminder inside the app. Removing the app from your device removes its local data.
