Free clean Support & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Free clean does not collect personal data.

  • Photo and video scanning is performed locally on the user's device.
  • Photos and videos are not uploaded to a server.
  • The app does not use advertising tracking.
  • The app does not sell, share, or transfer personal data.

The app requests access to the photo library only to show items for review and deletion. Deletion is performed through Apple's Photos permission flow.

Content Rights

The app interface and screenshots are original app materials. The app does not provide third-party media content.
