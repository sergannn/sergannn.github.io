Count Coffee

A simple local tracker for coffee cups, coffee-free days, and the money spent on the habit.

About

Count Coffee helps you mark cups in one tap, choose a price, track days without coffee, and see simple monthly statistics.

  • Daily coffee counter.
  • Monthly spending estimate.
  • Coffee-free day tracking.
  • Simple statistics by day.

Support

If you need help with Count Coffee, contact su.vasiliev@gmail.com.

Privacy

The app stores coffee entries locally on the user's device. It does not require an account, does not upload entries to a server, and does not use advertising tracking.
