Scam Pause Guard

Scam Pause Guard is a private checklist that helps you slow down before responding to suspicious calls, messages, payment links, and urgent requests.

How it works

Create a check, choose the scenario, write what the person asked for, and walk through three simple verification steps:

  • Use an official app, website, or phone number you already trust.
  • Ask a trusted person before acting.
  • Do not share codes, passwords, gift cards, crypto, or payment under pressure.

Privacy

Scam Pause Guard does not collect, sell, or share personal data. Checks are stored locally on your device. The app does not require an account and does not use a server.

Important note

The app does not automatically detect scams and does not replace your bank, law enforcement, or official fraud reporting. It is a private pause-and-check tool.
